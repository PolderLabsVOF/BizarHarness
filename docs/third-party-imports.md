# Third-party imported assets

## Specialist agents

The Bizar specialist-agent collection includes MIT-licensed source material,
copyright 2026 Affaan Mustafa, pinned at source revision
`22e8cf01d0b54719b3a49002fab2ccbda4ff5b9e`. Bizar preserves the specialist
role content under its native `bizar-` namespace, removes hard-coded Anthropic
model aliases, grants only portable Claude Code tools, and prepends Bizar
policy compatibility.

## Superpowers skills

The fourteen directories added to `config/skills/` derive from
[obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers) at commit
`b36e0829c6d0140e93cfef2ca599b1b07d4a7797`. The original assets are MIT
licensed, copyright 2025 Jesse Vincent. Bizar keeps the skill content and
supporting references, with a short compatibility notice that defers to Bizar
policy for approvals, routing, and worktree ownership.
